Before you start

Who can enter?
Anyone. One submission per person or team.
When can I start?
Any time from Sunday 15 November. Submit any time before the close.
Which model?
Any model, any public data, your own API key.
Can I change my call?
Until the close. The IPO price call must be in before the price is announced.
Does my code have to be public?
The judges must be able to read and rerun it. Public, or private with access for them.
What if the IPO slips?
The close and the scoring days move with it.
Is this investment advice?
No. Research and education only. AlphaStreet is not affiliated with Anthropic.
