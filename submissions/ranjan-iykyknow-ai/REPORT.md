## Limitations

- No live social data was collected for this frozen run; X credentials remain unverified in this environment.
- Hyperresearch was not installed or run; the compatible import adapter is supplied for future local research.
- Primary-source annotations were reviewed by an AI agent, not signed off by a human.
- Publication times on date-only sources are conservative end-of-New-York-day bounds, not invented precise publication times.
- Historical prices are a small purposive provider-published sample; full vintage point-in-time backtesting is unavailable.
- Model ranges are subjective scenario quantiles, not empirically calibrated 80% intervals.
- The diagnostic is conditional on actual offer prices and does not validate this final supply-aware model.
